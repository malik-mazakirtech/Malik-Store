Privacy Policy

How this demonstration website handles information.

1. Contact form

The demo form validates your entries in your browser. It does not send or save your name, email, or message.

2. Theme preference

Your light or dark theme preference is saved in localStorage on this browser. You can clear it through your browser settings.

3. Cookies and analytics

This version has no analytics scripts, tracking cookies, payment service, or account system.

4. External links

Email, phone and map links open external applications or services. Those services handle data according to their own policies.

5. Hosting

Your hosting provider may keep ordinary request logs. Review the hosting configuration before publishing.

6. Changes

Update this page whenever real forms, checkout, analytics or other services are introduced.
